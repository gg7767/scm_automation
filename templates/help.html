How it works

One short guide per workflow. If you're stuck on something not listed here, ask your SCM Head or Purchase Officer.

Raising an indent (site)
  1. Go to Indents → Raise indent.
  2. Pick a priority — Urgent notifies the approver immediately.
  3. Search for each item you need, set the quantity, and add it.
  4. Submit for approval once every item is listed.
Converting an approved indent to a PO (HO)
  1. Open the approved indent and click Convert to PO.
  2. Pick a vendor and enter how much of each item to order now — you can order less than the full quantity and convert the rest later, even to a different vendor.
  3. The draft PO opens next — add pricing, then submit for approval as usual.
Creating and approving a PO
  1. Purchase Orders → New PO, pick vendor and site.
  2. Add line items — search by item name/code; the rate auto-fills from an active rate contract if one exists.
  3. Submit for approval. Who can approve depends on the PO's total value (set up in Approval Rules).
  4. Once approved, mark it as sent (email/WhatsApp/manual) and download the PDF for the vendor.
Recording a delivery (GRN)
  1. GRNs → Record delivery, pick the PO the delivery is against.
  2. Enter the challan number/date and take a photo of the challan — required.
  3. Adjust received/accepted/rejected quantities per item if anything's short or damaged.
  4. Submit — this cannot be undone; a mistake needs a reversal GRN from HO.
Entering a vendor bill and matching it (Accounts)
  1. Bills → Enter bill, pick the PO, enter the vendor's invoice number/date and upload the scan.
  2. Adjust quantities/rates on each line to match the actual invoice, then click Run 3-way match.
  3. A clean match auto-approves for payment. A mismatch needs SCM Head to override with a reason, or send it back to the vendor via a remark.
Recording a payment (Accounts)
  1. Payments → Record payment — enter the vendor, amount, mode, and reference number.
  2. Open the payment and allocate it to one or more approved bills — a single payment can cover several bills, and an advance can sit unallocated until a bill arrives.
Issuing stock (site)
  1. Stock → Issue stock, pick a purpose, and list what's being used.
  2. Submit — this reduces stock immediately and cannot be undone (log a fresh issue if you made a mistake).
Logging machinery usage
  1. Open the machine under Machinery and use Deploy here to move it to your site.
  2. Add a daily usage log with hours run, fuel, and the operator's name.
